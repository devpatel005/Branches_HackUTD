# Stage 9 — Stretch Goals

Only after Stage 8 is done. Each item is independent; the team will pick which to build.

## A. Yearly wrap
"Simulate a year": advances plan month 0 by 12 using the engine's simulation (completed goals are paid from their balances; never reuse the research's buggy sample), then shows story-style recap cards (saved, interest earned, goals completed, tight months) and prompts for a new year-10 goal. Must keep determinism and goldens intact.

## B. Statement upload
Upload a 401(k)/brokerage statement screenshot; a Nemotron vision model extracts balance and contribution; the member confirms before saving. Extraction only, never calculation. See [[14 Research Notes#Nemotron]].

## C. Photon money thread
The bot creates a dedicated iMessage group per household (never joins personal chats). Supports life-event capture, quick affordability questions, and approvals via native polls, with deep links into the app. Shares the same chat history and agent. See [[14 Research Notes#Photon]]. If built and strong, consider swapping it in as the third Devpost track (OD-16).

## D. Push notifications
Session invites as real push notifications. Likely requires an Expo development build rather than Expo Go (verify).

## E. Households with more than two members
Ready check and presence for N members; UI for 3+ avatars.
